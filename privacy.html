Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

At Stateline Media, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal or operational data. This Privacy Policy outlines how our automated publishing application handles information when interfacing with social media APIs including TikTok, YouTube, and Meta.

1. Information We Access

Our desktop application and automated publishing workflows only access:

  • OAuth Access Tokens: Secure authorization tokens provided directly by social media platforms (TikTok, Google, Meta) to allow authorized video uploads.
  • Basic Account Information: Basic public profile identifiers (such as account username or channel ID) to verify upload destinations.
  • Video Media Files: Video and audio files explicitly selected and rendered on the local system for distribution.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store on external servers, or monetize any personal end-user browsing data, financial data, or sensitive personal data. The application operates strictly as a content distribution tool.

3. Data Usage & Platform Policy Compliance

All data accessed through third-party APIs (including TikTok API and YouTube API Services) is used strictly in compliance with:

  • TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Content Posting Guidelines
  • Google API Services User Data Policy
  • Meta Platform Terms

4. Data Storage and Retention

Authentication credentials and API tokens are retained locally in secured, encrypted storage within the local n8n automation engine on your machine. You may revoke access at any time through your respective platform security settings (e.g., TikTok Account Security / Google Permissions).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to accommodate new features or API requirements.

6. Contact Us

For any questions or privacy inquiries, please contact the Stateline Media team.
