Meta Platform Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform Terms (Section 4.d), Instagram Graph API guidelines, and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Stateline Media provides this transparent protocol for users wishing to request the deletion of any data associated with our automated content publishing and scheduling applications.

Key Summary: Stateline Media does not collect, sell, or maintain centralized databases of personal user accounts. All OAuth tokens and API credentials are kept locally on your client machine. Revoking app permissions immediately severs all access and deletes cached session data.

Option 1: Delete Data by Revoking App Permissions (Instant)

If you have authorized Stateline Media to publish content to your Facebook Page or Instagram Professional account, you can revoke permissions and delete your connection at any time:

For Facebook Pages:

  1. Log into your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, click on Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations).
  3. Locate Stateline Media (App ID: 1440455921266296) in the list of active applications.
  4. Click the Remove button.
  5. Check the box to confirm that you want to delete all posts, videos, or events published on your behalf, and click Remove.

For Instagram Accounts:

  1. Open the Instagram app or visit Instagram App Access Settings.
  2. Go to Settings > Website Permissions > Apps and Websites.
  3. Under the Active tab, find Stateline Media.
  4. Click Remove to instantly revoke access and erase token bindings.

Option 2: Submit a Manual Data Deletion Request

If you would like to request that any log files, cached identifiers, or analytics data associated with your social profile be purged manually by our engineering team:

  1. Send an email to: nusantarahealthapp@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Stateline Media - User Data Deletion Request
  3. Include your:
    • Facebook Page Name / Instagram Username
    • Approximate date of account authorization

Our team will process your request and complete the data purge within 48 business hours. You will receive an official confirmation code confirming that all session credentials and associated logs have been permanently erased.

Data Deletion Status Tracking

Every deletion request is logged with a unique confirmation identifier. To check the status of an ongoing deletion request, please reply to your initial request email or contact our support team.
